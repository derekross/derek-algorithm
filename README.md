# 🧠 The Derek Algorithm

**100% Open Source — MIT Licensed**

A social media ruleset for success, growth, and good vibes on Nostr.
It’s half serious, half satire — but it *works.*

---

### 1. **Be Social**

Don’t just post into the void. Reply to others, join conversations, and show up where the energy is. The algorithm rewards *connection*, not isolation.

### 2. **Engage and Interact**

Reactions, zaps, boosts, quotes, it’s all engagement. The more you genuinely interact, the more visible and memorable you become.

### 3. **Create Notes**

Your thoughts matter. Post your own content regularly, whether it’s original insights, memes, updates, or boosting and quoting other people’s great notes.
The key idea: **be a source of content**, not just a consumer.

### 4. **Send Zaps ⚡️**

Show appreciation, reward creativity, and spread sats like confetti. Zaps are the lifeblood of positive feedback loops on Nostr.

### 5. **Comment**

Don’t just react, contribute. Add value to conversations with humor, insight, or a fresh take. Comments are micro-content that keeps the feed alive and let others know that you are here and you want to engage.

### 6. **Send `myman.gif`**

A cultural ritual of solidarity and acknowledgment. Sometimes words aren’t enough, sometimes, you just need to send *myman.gif.*

### 7. **Be Active Often**

Consistency builds familiarity. Familiarity builds trust. Trust builds followers. Post, zap, reply, repeat.

### 8. **Rinse and Repeat (When Applicable)**

Social growth isn’t a one-time thing, it’s a rhythm. Keep the cycle going. When in doubt, go back to step one.
